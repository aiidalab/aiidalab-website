The AiiDAlab coding retreat brings developers and researchers together for an intensive week of focused work, shared learning, and open-source collaboration. Bring a project that would benefit from uninterrupted time and close collaboration.

Registration is a request. Both on-site and remote participation are limited to eight people. Confirmation will follow separately by email.

Proposed contributions

Participants are invited to share the projects and objectives they plan to work on during the retreat. The list will be updated as contributions are proposed.

Working notes and daily progress

How to reach Pietrasanta

Pietrasanta has a railway station within walking distance of the town centre. Train schedules and tickets are available from the Trenitalia website and the Trenitalia app.

Please check before travelling. The journey times below are indicative and based on the current timetable. Italian train timetables may be updated for September 2026; please check your complete journey on Trenitalia before departure.

From Pisa Airport — recommended

Take the PisaMover from the airport to Pisa Centrale. The journey takes approximately five minutes. From Pisa Centrale, direct regional trains to Pietrasanta typically take approximately 25–40 minutes.

From Florence Airport

Take tram line T2 from the airport to Firenze Santa Maria Novella railway station; the tram journey takes approximately 20 minutes. From Firenze Santa Maria Novella, continue by train to Pietrasanta. Depending on the connection, the train journey takes approximately 1 hour 30 minutes to 2 hours.

Alternatively, a taxi from the airport to Firenze Rifredi station may be convenient when it connects with a suitable train. Many trains towards Pietrasanta stop at Firenze Rifredi, but please check the departure station and timetable carefully.

From Genoa Airport

Genoa Airport is a possible alternative, although the onward journey is longer than from Pisa. The Airlink bus connects the airport with Genova Sestri Ponente Aeroporto railway station; alternatively, the Volabus connects the airport with Genova Piazza Principe and Genova Brignole stations. From Genoa, continue by train towards La Spezia and Pietrasanta. The journey normally takes approximately 2–3 hours and may require a change.

From Milan

The journey from Milano Centrale is relatively long. Current connections to Pietrasanta take approximately 3 hours 45 minutes or longer and require at least one change. Some long-distance trains stop at Massa Centro, which can be a useful alternative when a pickup has been arranged. From Massa Centro, Pietrasanta can also be reached by regional train or taxi.

Viareggio as an alternative arrival station

Some trains do not stop in Pietrasanta but do stop in Viareggio. Viareggio is approximately ten minutes from Pietrasanta by car, and regional trains connect Viareggio and Pietrasanta approximately every 30 minutes.

Complimentary pickup. A free pickup service from Pietrasanta, Massa Centro, and Viareggio railway stations will be organized for retreat participants. Pickup from Pisa Airport can also be arranged when genuinely necessary. All pickups must be coordinated directly with the organizers in advance.
